Pile shape

Walk a tape around the toe of the pile for the circumference, then throw it over the peak from toe to toe. No need to climb the pile.

Walk-around scan

Checking whether this phone supports AR…
  1. Toe: walk around the pile and tap where it meets the ground, every few metres (8+ taps).
  2. Surface: turn on Sweep and slowly pan the camera up and over every face you can see.
  3. Scale check (optional): lay a tape on the ground, tap Scale points, then tap both ends. Enter the tape distance after the scan to correct tracking drift.
  4. Finish: the app fits the ground, builds a surface and integrates the volume.
