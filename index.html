Loose stockpile densities vary with moisture and grading. Calibrate against a scale ticket once and the app remembers it.

Pile shape

Walk a tape around the toe of the pile for the circumference, then throw it over the peak from toe to toe. No need to climb the pile.

Walk-around scan

Checking whether this phone supports AR…
  1. Toe: walk around the pile and tap where it meets the ground, every 5–10 ft and at every bend (8+ taps). The outline closes itself and shades in. Tap the first point again, or tap Next, when you're back to the start. On uneven ground, the height of each toe tap is used, so tap right at the base all the way round.
  2. Surface: tap Next, turn on Sweep, and walk slowly around the pile 6–15 ft back, aiming at the faces and the top. Only points inside the toe outline and above the ground are kept. Tap Clear to start the surface again.
  3. Scale check (optional): lay a tape on the ground, tap Scale points, then tap both ends. Enter the tape distance after the scan to correct tracking drift.
  4. Finish: the app fits the ground, builds a surface and integrates the volume.

Sync between devices

Connected to . Syncs after every change and whenever you open the app.

Keeps projects, saved piles and density calibrations in a private GitHub repository, so your phone and PC show the same list. Set it up once on each device.

  1. Create a private GitHub repository named stockpile-data. Leave it empty.
  2. Create a fine-grained access token. Under repository access pick Only select repositories → stockpile-data. Under permissions set Contents: Read and write.
  3. Paste the token below and tap Connect. Do the same on your other devices.

The token stays on this device. It is not part of the app's code.
